# Time Tapestry brand revision, October 2, 2026

The first implementation was rejected as too flat, with uneven wordmark lettering. The interwoven two-t icon is explicitly approved and its geometry must remain unchanged.

## Build target

The supplied brand board is the primary visual source: warm natural photography, a carefully spaced rounded lowercase wordmark, white space, espresso/taupe gradients, sage and clay color fields, and the woven motif used at both large and small scales. This is a direct implementation of that supplied direction, not a new identity proposal.

## Guardrails

Keep all recording, saving, consent, review, address, email and mailing behavior intact. Never fabricate listening activity, camera previews or saved status. Decorative graphics must not compete with reading. Keep 16px minimum form text, visible focus, sufficient contrast and mobile stacking. Use existing generated illustrative photography; do not represent it as a real customer.

## Intended application

Homepage, setup/request, live interview, review, recipient story page, address page and shared footer. Shared wordmark exports also update email, print and film assets. Published previews and renders must be distinguished from templates.

## Landing story and motion, latest direction

The user selected the 21st.dev Siri Orb and Scroll Morph Hero and asked for one linear story serving people who share, request or sponsor a gift. The approved board remains the visual authority. The page moves from the emotional purpose to the guided conversation, the family collection, the postcard plan and three ways to begin. Church and organization gifting has an honest pilot information path; bulk purchasing does not exist yet.
