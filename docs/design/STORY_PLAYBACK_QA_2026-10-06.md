# Original-voice story playback QA

The real `StoryFilmPlayer` was bundled into a local browser fixture with fictional timed captions and a quiet 15-second test tone. No family media, provider sessions, email, or postcard calls were used.

Verified in Chrome through CUA:

- Playback begins only after the explicit Play tap. The media element reached readyState 4 and advanced its actual currentTime.
- The orb wrapper reads nonzero waveform amplitude from the playing audio. SiriOrb internals are unchanged.
- Making the story inactive pauses the audio. Backward seeking restores the earlier caption phrase.
- The complete spoken transcript opens as ordinary accessible text. Animated words are hidden from accessibility announcements.
- Forcing `createAnalyser` to throw left the native audio element playing and opened standard audio controls. Playback reached its natural end.
- The 390-pixel and 320-pixel viewport layouts have no horizontal overflow.
- AudioContext creation and resume are invoked synchronously within the Play gesture, before awaiting playback. Safari was reviewed in code, not tested on an iPhone or Safari browser.

The owner review, approved collection, and recipient collection now prefer the private chapter playback derivative. Verified legacy original-recording films still work. Approval submits all four exact playback hashes. Owners explicitly request optional MP4 export; recipients have no generation control and can download only an attached authorized export.

Validation: 23 focused tests passed across caption timing, original-voice review rendering, status polling, retry restrictions, chapter authorization, exact approval hashes, and opt-in exports. TypeScript completed without errors after the concurrent interview-tool fix.

## Four-part original recording review

A separate local fixture rendered the real `InterviewRecordingReview` and `LiveInterview` components with four fictional original takes. It blocked all provider and mutation requests. A small router adapter recorded the actual URLs generated by the components.

Verified in Chrome:

- All four chapter choices open the corresponding `media-q1` through `media-q4` original. Only one audio element is mounted at a time.
- The first original played the local test tone to its end with readyState 4.
- Review exposes no textarea, text-answer input, editable content, or default Continue link.
- Choosing Part 3 and Record again generates `/record/fixture-recording-review?key=fixture-owner&rerecord=q3`, shows the LiveInterview interface, and keeps Part 3 selected.
- Returning without starting a new provider session generates `/record/fixture-recording-review/review?key=fixture-owner&chapter=q3` and restores Part 3 with its original recording available.
- The real Next route was independently read: it uses LiveInterview unless `classic=1` is explicitly present. No classic flag is emitted by rerecord navigation.

The browser check found a retake display mismatch, which the LiveInterview owner corrected. A fresh bundle and browser recheck confirmed the Part 3 question now asks about helping others, the ready-state button says Back to review, and the camera-off caption matches Record this part again. Back to review successfully returned to Part 3 again.

Proofs: `evidence/front-door-2026-10-06-v1/recording-review-return-q3.jpg` and `evidence/front-door-2026-10-06-v1/recording-retake-q3-ready.jpg`.

Limitations: this tests real client components using a local router adapter, not the deployed Next server router. No new microphone recording, provider conversation, live upload, or submission was performed. The browser briefly stalled under concurrent build load; keyboard actions completed the checks after recovery.
