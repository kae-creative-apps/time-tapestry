# Current app logos

Start here for new work that needs to match timetapestry.app. These are unchanged production exports, copied from public/brand.

- lockup: the mark to the left of the two-line name.
- mark: the two interlocking t forms alone.
- wordmark: the two-line name alone.
- light: white artwork, intended for a dark background. A white logo may look blank in a white document viewer.
- SVG: editable vector paths, preferred for professional design and print.
- PNG: transparent raster, convenient for slides, email, or quick placement.

The production dark ink is #432E23. The older v39 kit uses #3B2A21 and different master geometry and composition. Do not silently mix their parts or describe them as identical masters. Keep proportions and spacing intact; do not retype or redraw the wordmark.
