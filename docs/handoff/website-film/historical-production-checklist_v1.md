# Time Tapestry film production checklist v1

October 3, 2026. The 86-second plan uses 18 seconds of original motion bookends and 68 seconds of product demonstration. This allocation is our production decision. It is not a Gloo rule.

## Current status and release gates

- [ ] Root: Record one complete working collection, from saved answer through review, approval, recipient access, and reply. Use permission-cleared demonstration material and label illustrative identities.
- [ ] Root: Capture the product's actual ElevenLabs guided interview or another verified product AI interaction. Generating the film's narrator with AI alone does not demonstrate the product's AI contribution. The primary script does not require Gloo credentials or attribute the story flow to Gloo.
- [ ] Tayloe/team: Confirm the intended Gloo track. Track choice changes the evidence and additional documents required.
- [ ] Root: Verify each screenshot or capture against the exact narration. An animated UI recreation helps explain a flow but is not proof that the application performed it.
- [ ] Root: Generate narration using the interview agent's exact existing ElevenLabs voice ID. Confirm the selected voice rather than replacing it with a similar voice.
- [ ] Team: Confirm rights for that voice and any identifiable recorded person. Obtain the written releases the rules require before submission.
- [ ] Root: Measure every generated narration segment against its scene. Keep natural delivery and breathing room. The primary script is 140 words. Its optional Gloo alternative is 145 words.
- [ ] Root: Regenerate affected narration and captions if changing a narration variant. The Gloo alternatives are optional and require recorded evidence of those specific provider operations. They are not a universal hackathon eligibility requirement.
- [ ] Root: Review a full export at normal speed with sound, then verify its duration with file metadata. The project target is exactly 86 seconds; the official maximum is 90 seconds.

## Official requirements verified from Gloo

Source: [Official Rules, updated September 29, 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jmH6lnQkI_B9YQeHT12pCanQI8W4yoE_yK3EO2IWaeQ/edit). The research team read the current source on October 3.

- [ ] Show a working application in a demo video no longer than 90 seconds.
- [ ] Upload through the Google Drive folder supplied through the organizer's submission form. A YouTube or Vimeo link is not a verified substitute.
- [ ] Submit other required materials, including the solution/code through the designated GitHub and/or Drive route and a 250-word English solution description.
- [ ] Preliminary submission: October 7 at 11 p.m. Eastern. Video optional at this stage; other materials required.
- [ ] Finalist submission: October 8 at 11 a.m. Eastern. Video required.
- [ ] Prepare the separate 90-second solution summary. At least one finalist team member must attend and present onsite.
- [ ] Identify paid art/audio assets and their sources. Make original motion and use the reference films for principles, not footage, artwork, scripts, logos, or music.
- [ ] Obtain written permission for each identifiable person seen or heard, covering the promotional use required by Gloo's rules.

Generative creation tools are allowed. The five judging categories each carry 20%: Concept/Product, Innovation, Impact and Execution, AI, and Presentation. The film should show the product's human purpose, a coherent working flow, actual AI contribution, and a legible outcome. The rules do not establish that this particular application automatically fits a track.

## Track-dependent requirements

Source: [Current Challenge Summary](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MQFKKmx24C7yCXgNhWscPn4cE3aoYKpat79Ro3SCyY0/edit), linked from [Hackathon HQ](https://gloo.com/ai/hackathon/hq).

- [ ] If entering Agents of Flourishing, prepare a live agent run using input the judges can see and an edge case. The recorded film does not replace the live run.
- [ ] For that track, prepare the Agent Build Document with architecture, verbatim prompts and earlier versions, stack, permissions, evaluation and auditable session log, human handoff, reproduction steps, and known gaps.
- [ ] If considering Ministry Resourcing, verify alignment with the donor-story challenge. A family gift product is not automatically a donor-campaign system.
- [ ] If considering the Bible track, verify that Scripture is essential and the required YouVersion core is present. Do not claim this from faith-oriented wording alone.
- [ ] If considering Track 4, confirm the organizer's current route and criteria before asserting eligibility.

## Production recommendations, not official technical rules

- [ ] Export a 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio. The public rules did not specify these technical settings.
- [ ] Include readable captions and preserve UI text at normal viewing size. Captions are a recommendation, not a verified requirement.
- [ ] Keep a four-second margin below the official maximum and retain a local backup.
- [ ] Check the actual submission form before delivery for file-size limits, naming, upload permissions, and any additional export instructions. These details have not been verified publicly.
- [ ] Keep the actual answer, returned follow-up or prepared example, draft, saved edit, approval, and recipient outcome legible long enough to understand.
- [ ] Label shortened processing waits. Avoid decorative cursor activity or interface motion that obscures the action.
- [ ] Use Quicksand and Manrope plus the approved Time Tapestry palette: espresso `#3B2A21`, peach `#B98B74`, moss `#868973`, walnut `#6A5747`, and white `#FFFFFF`.
- [ ] Preserve the approved two-t icon and postcard identity. Let one story/postcard object connect the scenes, following the clarity of Webflow and the continuity of Mailchimp without copying their artwork.
- [ ] Build the master in Remotion. Use another tool only for a segment or finishing task that improves the result, rather than maintaining competing master edits.
- [ ] Use understated paper and interface sounds when licensed or original. Keep the narrator easy to hear and avoid a dated or sentimental music treatment.

## Claims to keep out unless independently verified

Do not attribute a mock or source-text fallback to live Gloo or another AI provider. A real source-text preparation flow can demonstrate that answers become stories without claiming live AI generation. Do not promise permanent storage, completed postal fulfillment, playable family films, venue-network reliability, or a public demo URL without confirming the specific evidence. Keep all four stories available together. A postcard can invite someone back to a collection; it does not gradually unlock the collection.

The primary narration is ready to build the working-product film. Actual captured application actions, a verified product AI interaction, rights clearance, and the selected track's requirements must be checked before the result is described as hackathon-ready. Gloo credentials are necessary only if the film specifically demonstrates and names a live Gloo integration.
