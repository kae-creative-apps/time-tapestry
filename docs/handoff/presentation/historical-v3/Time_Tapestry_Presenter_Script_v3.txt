TAYLOE
A family member can ask these questions. Time Tapestry helps them follow through: answer when you can, review four chapters, and share them with family. The postcards are designed to invite people back to those stories and encourage a response. Our aim is to help families pass on faith and generosity.

WHY NOT INTERVIEW THEM YOURSELF?
We encourage that. The gap we are testing is follow-through when distance or schedules get in the way. Time Tapestry gives someone a place to start, the ability to answer over time, control over what they share, and a finished collection their family can return to. We will measure whether that leads to completed stories and meaningful conversations.

HOW DO YOU KNOW THERE IS A MARKET?
Marco Polo makes asynchronous connection familiar. Storyworth and Remento show paid family-storytelling offers exist. Neither proves demand for our exact product. Our hypothesis is that Christian legacy, personal encouragement, postcards and a reply loop are worth choosing and paying for. We need a real family purchase or sponsor commitment, along with completion and cost evidence, to establish that.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT?
AI interviews, written chapters, QR recordings and instant family sharing already exist. Our proposed distinction is the specific Christian legacy experience: four focused chapters about lived values, the storyteller’s own encouragement or Scripture, quarterly physical reminders and a family response. This combination is a positioning hypothesis, not a defensible monopoly claim.
