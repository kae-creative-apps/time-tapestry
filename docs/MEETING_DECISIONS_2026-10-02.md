## The agreed experience

A person gives or requests a Time Tapestry story. The storyteller answers four core questions by typing, voice or video, with optional follow-ups. They can pause, return, rerecord and choose from saved takes. Four written chapters and postcard notes are drafted from the selected answers. Recorded answers need an edited video and human review before they are presented as finished videos. The storyteller approves the actual package before any recipient sharing or mailing.

The first postcard introduces the gift. Its QR link opens **all four approved chapters and any included finished videos immediately**. Four distinct postcards arrive on a planned 0, 3, 6 and 9 calendar-month schedule. Later cards revisit the story; they do not unlock content. Each can include the storyteller's own encouragement or Scripture. Fourteen days after confirmed mailing, an email offers the link and an optional reply. The storyteller receives an email when a reply is sent.

## Evidence and pitch decisions

Lead with **relationships and character**, including generosity. Purpose, meaning and faith provide supporting context. These are intended contributions to flourishing, not measured results. Pilot measures should include interview completion, approved stories, chapter viewing, replies and self-reported meaningful family conversations. None establishes increased giving without a suitable study.

Three defensible context statements for the deck:

- **43%** of Americans 50+ named life lessons and values among important things they wanted to leave behind. This is Edward Jones/Age Wave's 2021 research, not an AARP statistic. [Primary press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pandemic-prompted-first-time-legacy-planning-conversations-for-44-5-million-americans-edward-jones-finds-301398039.html).
- **51%** of surveyed Protestant senior pastors were very concerned about younger Christians' future financial support. The pastor survey was conducted in 2022 and published in 2023. It measures concern, not proof that younger people lack generosity. [Barna](https://www.barna.com/research/future-generosity/).
- Parent-child conversations about giving were associated with a greater likelihood of children giving in the Women Give study. This is context for the conversation approach, not evidence that this product changes behavior. [Indiana University](https://philanthropy.indianapolis.iu.edu/news-events/news/_news/2013/women-give-2013.html).

The [current official rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jmH6lnQkI_B9YQeHT12pCanQI8W4yoE_yK3EO2IWaeQ/edit), exported October 2, assign **20% each** to Concept/Product, Innovation, Impact and Execution, AI, and Presentation. Older weighted summaries in the project are superseded. Demo video is at most **90 seconds**. Kaelyn handles the form; no form or entry was submitted by this work.
