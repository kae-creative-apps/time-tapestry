**HOW WILL ENTRIES BE JUDGED?**  
   
The judging period will begin on Wednesday, October 7\.  If you/your team is eligible to present, your team must present your entry onsite in the presentation session. You and/or a member of your team may be asked by the judges to clarify your entry, verify the work completed during the competition period, or discuss the validity of the solution. The highest-scoring entries who will continue to the finals will be announced that day.  
   
There will be two rounds of judging by a panel of judges: preliminary judging in tracks on Wednesday night, and finalist judging in front of the full group on Thursday morning. There will also be an independent code verification team who checks and scores any related code of submissions.  
   
Entries will be judged on the following criteria and factors:  
   
**Scoring.** Each judge scores each Entry on each of the five criteria below on a scale of 0 to 10\. Each criterion score is multiplied by the weight shown for that criterion, and the weighted scores are summed to produce that judge’s total for the Entry. An Entry’s score for a round is the arithmetic mean of all judges’ totals for that Entry, calculated to two decimal places. The weights below total 100%. Judges score only the criteria listed below. Gloo will publish any change to these criteria or weights no later than 11:59 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time on Monday, September 7, 2026, after which they will not change.  
 

1. Concept/Product \- 25% of total score  
   1. Does the product address the challenge?  
   2. How user-friendly is the approach?  
   3. How much progress did they make during the event?  
2. Innovation \- 20% of total score  
   1. Does the project portray a creative approach to the issue?  
   2. Does the project address an actual need?  
   3. Did they start from scratch or build on existing technology?  
3. Impact and Execution \- 25% of total score  
   1. How much impact (quality and quantity) could the solution have?  
   2. Could it produce a big change or a little change to the problem it addresses?  
   3. Is this solution implementable?  
   4. How hard would it be to scale?  
4. AI  \- 20% of total score  
   1. How did the project use AI?  
5. Presentation \- 10% of total score  
   1. How well did the team communicate with each other and the audience?  
   2. How well did they respond to questions from judges?

   
**TIE-BREAKING PROCEDURE**  
   
If two or more Entries receive identical round scores and the tie affects advancement to the finals or the award of any prize, the tie will be resolved by applying the following, in order, until the tie is broken: (1) the higher mean weighted score on Impact and Execution; (2) then the higher mean weighted score on Concept/Product; (3) then the higher mean weighted score on AI; (4) then the higher mean weighted score on Innovation; (5) then the higher mean weighted score on Presentation; and (6) then the determination of the Head Judge, a single individual designated by Gloo in writing before judging begins, who will resolve the tie by applying the published criteria and will record the basis for the determination in writing.  
   
**No tie affecting advancement to the finals or the award of any prize will be resolved by drawing lots, coin toss, random selection, or any other method involving chance.**  
   
**Verification of Winners.** All potential winners are subject to verification, and completion of verification is a condition precedent to any award. Any announcement of a winner at the event is provisional until verification is complete. Gloo may require a potential winner to present the Entry and respond to questions from the judges or the code verification team, to verify the work completed during the Competition Period, to provide proof of identity and of age, to confirm compliance with the in-person attendance requirement, and to provide the documentation described in the Taxes section.
