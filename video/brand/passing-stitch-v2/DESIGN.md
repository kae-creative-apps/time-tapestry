# Passing the stitch, v2

Motion direction for review. October 5, 2026.

## Choreography

1. Establish the first t with a connected upright and foot.
2. Let its curved crossbar lead into the second crossbar. The original paper seam remains open.
3. Complete the receiving t from the crossing outward, then downward into its foot.
4. Settle the full icon into a centered lockup and reveal the wordmark as one piece.
5. Reveal “Stories woven together.” and hold completely still.

Duration: 6.5 seconds, including a quiet final hold. Silent. This is a reviewable study, not a new deployed film closer. A shorter production cut can follow approval. A future webpage embed should show the completed still when reduced motion is requested and play only once when introduced.

## Boundaries

The animation reveals the original paths with masks. It does not deform, redraw, rotate, bounce, split, or replace the logo. Passing something on does not erase the first t. Meaning comes from the order in which the mark appears.

The finished frame must preserve the approved flat silhouette. Check the central seam, all four terminal shapes, wordmark bounds, and reading time before delivery. Review intermediate frames for detached fragments and review the continuous motion for the handoff.
