# Time Tapestry woven logo, v1

## Visual identity
Use the approved v39 native icon contours and outlined stacked wordmark, copied without geometry edits from docs/handoff/brand/kit-v39. This is a new motion study, not a replacement for the deployed story-film closer.

## Colors
Paper #fbfaf8 is the canvas. Approved espresso #3B2A21 is the logo and final type. Walnut #6A5747 gives fibers depth. Peach #B98B74 and moss #868973 may appear only as individual quiet tracer threads. White #FFFFFF is a small fiber highlight. Tonal shades are lighting, not a new brand palette.

## Typography
Wordmark stays outlined approved v39 artwork, never retyped. The exact approved line, Stories woven together., uses the bundled Quicksand font.

## Motion
Ten seconds, 1920 by 1080, 30 fps. Two loose bundles introduce the material. Warp and weft progressively fill the exact four icon pieces. A gentle camera pullback gives the mark room alongside the outlined wordmark. Tagline settles beneath the completed lockup. Final two seconds are a calm hold.

## Material
Fine alternating over-under yarn, subtle bevel highlights and contact shadows. A bright warm paper field. No additional decorative pattern, fake grain overlay, metallic shine, neon, or stock images. Silent motion study, no narration or music.

## Reuse
Authoring works in Remotion and HyperFrames with the same SVG geometry and timing specification. Keep v1 isolated; never overwrite an approved closer or automate a deployment as part of this study.
