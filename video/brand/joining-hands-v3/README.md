# Joining hands, v3

Review study, October 5, 2026. Tayloe's direction: the end of one t meets the other like joining hands, making the passing-down narrative visible.

Both complete forms begin slightly apart. Their curved crossbar ends reach toward each other, meet for half a second, then interlace as the two forms settle into the exact approved mark. The wordmark and “Stories woven together.” appear after the gesture. Silent, seven seconds, espresso on paper.

## Motion

- 0.05 to 0.45 seconds: the two complete t forms appear.
- 0.55 to 1.7 seconds: their bodies approach and the two crossbar ends reach.
- 1.7 to 2.2 seconds: the tips meet around native icon coordinates (229, 207.5). Hold the contact so it is perceptible.
- 2.2 to 3.25 seconds: the temporary bends relax while the forms interlace into the approved stitch.
- 3.25 to 4.75 seconds: settle into the lockup and show the original wordmark and tagline.
- 4.75 to 7 seconds: hold the exact finished identity.

Only the distal crossbars bend temporarily. The upright and lower-stem contours retain their original geometry. All offsets and deformations reach exactly zero before the closing hold. The original v39 path tags and outlined wordmark are reused from `../woven-logo-v1/artwork.mjs`. No masks draw the logo on, and no new hand or finger illustration is introduced. A temporary paper seam keeps the over/under relationship readable during the interlace, then disappears.

## Preview and render

Run from the repository root:

```sh
npx remotion studio video/brand/joining-hands-v3/entry.tsx --no-open --port 3226
```

Open `http://localhost:3226/TimeTapestryJoiningHands`.

```sh
node video/brand/joining-hands-v3/render.mjs
```

Exports inspection frames and `video/output/joining-hands-v3/Time-Tapestry-Joining-Hands-v3.mp4`. Use `--stills` for frames alone. The renderer refuses to overwrite an existing MP4.
