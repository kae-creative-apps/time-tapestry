# Time Tapestry video direction

## Style prompt

A restrained family documentary. The person's face and voice carry the story. Titles and captions clarify the story without competing with it. This prototype carries the current application's palette forward, as defined in tailwind.config.ts. It is not an approved new identity.

## Colors

- Paper #faf6ef: title background and readable light text.
- Ink #1a1714: video canvas and caption background.
- Oxblood #7a2e2e: small brand detail.
- Muted ink #6b6358: secondary title text.

## Typography

Use Georgia for the temporary title wordmark and Arial for captions. These deterministic fallbacks avoid a remote font dependency. Replace with licensed, locally bundled Fraunces and Inter after brand approval.

## Motion

A short fade and 18px rise for titles. An understated line reveals beneath the temporary wordmark. No movement over a speaking face. Captions remain steady. The closer lasts four seconds.

## What not to do

- Do not replace the person with an avatar or cloned voice.
- Do not add decorative stock footage or synthetic family photographs.
- Do not remove a pause merely because it is quiet.
- Do not claim this temporary text wordmark is an approved logo.
- Do not generate new wording or new cuts during playback.
