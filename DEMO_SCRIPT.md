# Time Tapestry — 90-Second Demo Script

## Timing

Total: 90 seconds
Pace: unhurried, warm, one thought at a time

---

## 0–10s: The Problem

> **"Religion giving is the only major philanthropic sector in decline. Donor participation fell 3.6% last year."**
>
> But the most powerful proof of impact isn't a report. It's a relationship. It's a story.

**What to show:** Landing page at `/`

**Action:** Pause on the headline. Let the stat land.

---

## 10–20s: The Product

> Time Tapestry captures an older person's story of generosity and weaves it into a legacy for the next generation.
>
> Two paths: request a story, or share my own.

**What to show:** Landing page CTAs

**Action:** Point to "Request a story" and "Share my story." Then say, "Let's share one."

---

## 20–40s: The Live Interview

> Watch what happens when Eleanor sits down to share her story.

**What to show:** `/share`

**Action:** Enter Eleanor's name and Sammie as the recipient. Start the interview.

> The AI interviewer greets her, asks the first question, listens, responds warmly, and asks a follow-up.
>
> The AI interviewer uses questions grounded in research on how values actually transmit across generations.

**What to show:** `/interview/{id}`

**Action:** Let Eleanor answer one or two questions. Show the warm, natural follow-up.

---

## 40–60s: The Story Generation

> Skip ahead. The AI weaves the transcript into four chapters, preserving Eleanor's exact words.

**What to show:** `/review/{id}` or a pre-seeded review page

**Action:** Scroll through the four chapters quickly.

> Her grandchild, Sammie, receives this ... a private digital keepsake with the full story, audio narration, and the causes Eleanor believes in.

**What to show:** `/keepsake/demo-gigi`

**Action:** Scroll slowly. Highlight the welcome note, the values, and the causes.

---

## 60–80s: The Physical + Family Concept

> And in production, five postcards arrive in the mail over five weeks ... each one carrying a piece of the story, with a QR code back to the digital keepsake.

**What to show:** `/postcards-demo`

**Action:** Flip through the postcard stack.

> Families build a shared tapestry over time. Grandparents, parents, children ... each adds their thread.

**What to show:** `/family/demo-family`

**Action:** Show multiple stories linked together.

---

## 80–90s: The Nonprofit Model

> Nonprofits and churches sponsor these for their top donors. The nonprofit sees aggregate engagement ... never personal stories. The families own their own data.

**What to show:** `/org`

**Action:** Point to the privacy promise and the nonprofit dashboard.

---

## Closing Line

> Time Tapestry. Weaving the stories that matter.

**What to show:** Return to landing page `/`
